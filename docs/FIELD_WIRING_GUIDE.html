Field box: power & alligator-clip wiring

The one that trips people up: the negative uses a Wago, the positive (12V) does not; it hops across the relay COM screws instead.

The big idea: the 12 V goes OUT through the relays to the firework and comes BACK through one shared Wago. So you have one Wago (the negative) collecting all the returns, and the positive spread across the 8 relay COM screws by little jumpers. Each firework sits in the middle, held by two alligator clips.

The whole loop, one channel

Follow the red line: battery + → fuse → relay COM → NO → clip A → steel wool. Then the black line: steel wool → clip B → NEG Wago → battery −. When the relay clicks, that loop completes and the strand burns.

1. The NEGATIVE Wago: what goes in (you already did this)

One shared junction. A 5-slot Wago holds 5 wires; chain a second one for the rest.

2. The POSITIVE: no Wago, it hops across the COM screws

The 12V positive is not collected in a Wago. It's fed to relay COM1 and then daisy-chained screw-to-screw across all 8 COMs.

StepWire
1Battery + → fuse → relay COM1 screw
2Short 20AWG jumper: COM1 → COM2
3Repeat: COM2→COM3, COM3→COM4 … COM7→COM8 (7 jumpers total)
4Middle COM screws hold two wires (jumper in + jumper out). Twist the two stripped ends together before clamping so both stay put

Would a Wago work for the positive too? Yes. Instead of the daisy chain you can run battery+ → fuse → a second Wago, then one wire from that Wago to each COM. Same result, just more wire. The daisy chain uses fewer parts, so most people do that.

3. Where the alligator clips go

Two clips per firework, one on each end of the steel-wool strand.

ClipWired fromTo
Clip A (hot)relay NO of that channelone end of the steel wool
Clip B (return)the NEG Wagothe other end of the steel wool

So each channel needs a lead from its NO ending in clip A, and a lead from the NEG Wago ending in clip B. The strand bridges the two clips; that's what the relay switches on and the sense circuit reads.

Fuse safety: the fuse is the master kill. Leave it out of the holder while wiring and clipping strands. With no fuse, the whole 12V positive chain is dead. Insert it only when everyone is clear and you're ready to fire, then pull it right back out.
