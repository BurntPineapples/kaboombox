Firework controller · panel box

Controller Rewire Sheet

Every wire from the panel parts to the ESP32 terminal board and the breadboard, in the order to connect them. Tick each wire as it goes in. Your ticks are saved on this device only.

Before you start: batteries out of the controller, and the fuse out of the field box. Leave the fuse out until every check below passes.

The two red rails carry different voltages. Left red = 3.3 V, right red = 6 V from the batteries. Never connect them together. Put a bit of tape on the right one marked “6V”.

Batteries: rechargeable NiMH AAs (4.8 V) are kinder to the LCD than fresh alkalines (up to 6.4 V, just over the LCD's 5 V rating). Alkalines work, the LCD just runs a little over spec.

Wire colours (suggested)

Use any colours you have; sticking to one colour per job makes it easy to fix later. Put a tape flag on each wire end (B3, CH5, KEY L…).

Resistors

Which resistor is which

Only two values in this box. Quick tell: stripes start red-red = 220Ω, start brown-black = 10k.

Read from the end away from the gold band (on blue 5-band ones, away from the lone band with the bigger gap). Not sure? Multimeter on Ω: about 0.22k = 220Ω, about 10.0k = 10k. Don't hold both legs with your fingers while measuring. Keep the 100k ones (brown-black-yellow) for the field box; on an LED they'd barely glow.

How power flows

Three rails on the breadboard

4×AA pack +→POWER toggle→right red rail · 6V→ESP 5V + LCD VCC
ESP 3V3→left red rail · 3.3V→key switch + four 10k pull-ups
pack −, ESP GND, LCD GND, every button and LED→both blue rails · GND

The battery pack's own little switch stays ON; the panel toggle does the switching.

Map 1

ESP32 terminal board

Laid out like your photo: USB on the left. Coloured terminals get a wire; grey ones stay empty. If the order on your board differs, go by the printed label, not the position.

P36 and P39 may be printed as VP / VN (or SVP / SVN). The 5V terminal may say VIN.

Map 2

Breadboard

Only rows 1–47 are used. Each channel LED sits on one row: ESP wire in column a, a 220Ω resistor jumping the middle gap from e to f, LED wire out of j. Buttons 5–8 each get a row with a 10k resistor up to the 3.3V rail.

If the printed rail lines have a break halfway down, the rail is split there: bridge each rail across the break with a short jumper.

Last step

Live test with the field box

  1. Controller and field box both on, field fuse still out. LCD shows DISARMED and LINK green is lit.
  2. Clip a test igniter (or a bulb) onto field channel 1. Channel LED 1 on the panel lights within a second, and the LCD ready count goes up.
  3. Turn the field box off. Within about 2 seconds LINK red lights and the LCD says LINK DOWN.
  4. Only after all of that: key to DISARMED, fuse back in.
